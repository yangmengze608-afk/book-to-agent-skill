# Style Dimensions

优先写“看得见、能被生成模型执行”的特征。

## 1. Medium / rendering
- photography
- cinematic still
- 3D render
- clay / plush / felt
- vector illustration
- ink wash
- watercolor
- gouache
- colored pencil
- pixel art
- collage
- mixed media

记录：媒介 + 完成方式 + 数字/实体质感。

## 2. Shape language
- rounded / blocky / angular / elongated
- soft silhouette / hard geometric silhouette
- exaggerated head-body ratio
- short limbs / oversized hands
- simplified anatomy / realistic anatomy

## 3. Line & edge
- no visible outline
- thin clean contour
- rough dry-brush edge
- soft atmospheric edge
- hard cel-shaded edge
- variable line weight

## 4. Composition
- centered / off-center
- symmetry / asymmetry
- large negative space
- subject occupancy ratio
- horizon placement
- foreground occlusion
- layered depth
- poster-like flat staging

## 5. Camera / perspective
- eye level / low angle / high angle
- wide / normal / telephoto feel
- orthographic / isometric
- shallow / deep perspective
- compressed space
- fisheye / distortion
- close-up / medium / wide / extreme wide

不要虚构具体焦段；只有证据充分时再给近似范围。

## 6. Lighting
- soft overcast
- hard noon sun
- warm practicals
- rim light
- backlight
- volumetric beams
- low-key / high-key
- diffuse shadow
- crisp shadow
- motivated light sources

## 7. Palette
不要只列颜色名称，记录“颜色之间的关系”：

- low saturation cool base + warm accent
- monochrome with one accent hue
- pastel high-key
- deep blue/gray shadows + amber highlights
- complementary teal/orange
- analogous warm reds/oranges

## 8. Material / texture
- matte plastic
- soft fur
- glossy ceramic
- rough paper grain
- brushed metal
- wet asphalt reflection
- canvas texture
- film grain

## 9. Depth / focus
- shallow DOF
- all-in-focus graphic look
- haze separation
- atmospheric perspective
- bokeh character
- foreground blur

## 10. Detail density
- icon-like minimal
- simplified readable forms
- medium environmental detail
- dense microtexture
- hyper-detailed surface realism

## 11. Atmosphere
Atmosphere 只能作为辅助维度，必须尽量落回具体视觉证据。

例如：
- “孤独” → single small subject + large empty dark environment + distant practical light
- “治愈” → rounded forms + warm diffuse light + low contrast + soft materials
- “压迫” → low ceiling / tight crop + high contrast + deep shadow + looming perspective

## 12. Motion language
- frozen crisp action
- long-exposure trails
- subtle motion blur
- floating particles
- still tableau
- elastic cartoon motion

## 13. Recurring motifs
只记录视觉语法，不把特定 IP / 人物写进核心 Style DNA。

可记录：
- tiny subject inside oversized environment
- circular props repeated across scenes
- window-frame foreground motif
- glowing particles around subject

## 14. Negative evidence
同样重要：系列反复“没有”的东西。

例如：
- no hard black outlines
- no pure white background
- no glossy photoreal skin
- no crowded composition
- no text overlay
